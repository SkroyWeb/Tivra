The Existing Gap

Identifying critical deficiencies in modern engineering education curricula.

03

Current Challenges

Modern engineering education often faces the following challenges:

Limited exposure to real-world systems design

Project work focused more on submission than impact

Lack of structured cross-disciplinary collaboration

Minimal integration between problem identification and implementation

As a result, students graduate with theoretical competence but limited applied confidence. Skroy Nova seeks to introduce a structured environment where real challenges are engineered into practical solutions under academic mentorship.
