Skroy Nova is currently in its conceptual stage, developed with the intention of strengthening applied engineering culture within our academic environment.

While theoretical knowledge forms the backbone of engineering education, there remains a gap between classroom learning and real-world problem solving. Skroy Nova aims to bridge this gap by creating a structured, faculty-guided innovation platform where students work on practical, implementation-driven challenges.

This document outlines the vision, structure, and proposed framework of the initiative. It is presented for academic review, refinement, and mentorship guidance before any formal launch.

“This is not an event proposal — it is the blueprint of a long-term innovation ecosystem.”
