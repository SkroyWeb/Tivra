Operational Model

How Skroy Nova Will Function

Execution Framework

01

Problem Identification

  • Real-world institutional or community problems defined.
  • Scope and constraints clearly documented for clarity.
02

Proposal Submission

  • Teams submit technical approach.
  • Feasibility analysis & cost estimation.
  • Detailed implementation roadmap.
03

Evaluation & Shortlisting

  • Academic review panel evaluation.
  • Assessment of technical viability.
  • Top teams selected for prototype development.
04

Development Stage

  • Teams work under faculty mentorship.
  • Periodic technical reviews conducted.
  • Iterative refinement of prototypes.
05

Final Demonstration

  • Working model presented to panel.
  • Evaluation based on practicality.
  • Scalability & sustainability assessment.
