Role of Faculty Mentors

Academic Advisory Council

Guiding Innovation with
Academic Integrity

Skroy Nova will function under the guidance of a Faculty Advisory Council. This ensures that while student initiative drives the project, academic rigor shapes the outcome.

"Faculty involvement will remain advisory and review-based, ensuring minimal operational burden. Academic integrity and institutional alignment will remain central to the initiative."

Proposed Responsibilities

Technical Validation

Reviewing and validating problem statements for academic relevance.

Feasibility Review

Assessing the practical viability of proposed student solutions.

Ethical Standards

Ensuring all projects adhere to institutional ethical guidelines.

Structured Feedback

Providing mentorship during critical development stages.

Evaluation Panels

Participating in the final assessment of project outcomes.
