Year 1: Pilot Model

A controlled launch strategy to test structure, refine governance, and ensure operational feasibility.

09

Operational Framework

The initial launch will be strictly defined by the following parameters:

Limited to one defined real-world problem

Restricted cohort of 8–10 participating teams

Application of a structured evaluation framework

Establishment of documented learning outcomes

Primary Objective To test the operational structure, refine governance mechanisms, and ensure long-term feasibility before scaling to a larger institutional level.
